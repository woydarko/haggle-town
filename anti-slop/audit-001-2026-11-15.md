# antislop audit 001: landing page

Date: 2026-11-15. Scope: `web/index.html`, `web/src/landing/*` (commit range e194e96..HEAD). Mode 2 (after).
Direction source: `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` (warm market bazaar, parchment, stamped prices, chalk price board, deal tape, shopkeeper bubbles).

Design Read: game landing page for hackathon judges and Roblox players, warm market-bazaar language, target dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.
Current output reads as ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2: sections are uniform, which contradicts the target.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Lock = LOW.
Note: the LOW items (10 to 15) are what make the page read as AI-made at a glance.

## HIGH

1. **R-27 UI states.** Live data has no loading or error state. The deal ticker and merchant numbers simply vanish while loading or when the API fails. Fix: "Checking the stalls..." while loading, "Market unreachable, numbers paused" on error.
2. **R-25 contrast.** Step numerals `.step-n` are #D9C7A6 on #FFF9EE = 1.58:1 (fails even the 3:1 large-text bar). Nav links are white straight over the hero photo, guarded only by a text shadow; the cloud area was never checked.
3. **R-35 verification.** No recorded click-through of every control (nav anchors, 4 CTAs, coin cards, 6 FAQ items, footer links, merchant cards by keyboard).
4. **R-36 honesty (decorative status dot).** A pulsing green "live" dot sits next to a deal from 23 h ago. It claims a liveness the data does not have.

## MEDIUM

5. **R-14 identical cards.** Steps, benefits, coins and merchants all use the same `.lcard` (2 px border, 12 px radius, same hover lift). No hierarchy between a merchant and a bullet list.
6. **R-19 template motion.** Every block fades up, every card lifts on hover, the sign sways forever and the dot pulses forever. Two endless loops with no trigger.
7. **R-12 shadow.** Shadows on nav, sign, hero card, portraits, CTA card and every hovered card: the page floats.
8. **R-06 typography.** `TRADES WOOD` style labels: uppercase with wide tracking.
9. **R-04 / R-22 filler imagery.** Steps 3 and 4 reuse the coin image as a stand-in icon. Backer coins shows the same "H" coin three times for three different coins.

## LOW (most visible)

10. **R-05 template layout.** Order is the AI default: hero with 2 CTAs, numbered "how it works" cards, benefits grid, FAQ, CTA card, footer. Every section is centered heading + lead + card grid.
11. **R-20 identity motif missing.** DESIGN_SYSTEM names a chalk price board, a tape of recent deals and shopkeeper speech bubbles. The page uses only the bubbles; swap the logo and it could be any product.
12. **C-3 template section.** "Why a living market" repeats what the hero and FAQ already say.
13. **R-16 / copy rhythm.** Rule of three everywhere ("a purse, a stall and a temper"; "watch prices move, check every purse and follow each deal"; two lists of exactly three bullets), staccato negations ("No wallet, no seed phrase, no fee popups."), instruction copy ("Hover or focus to hear a line"), stock heading ("Meet the merchants").
14. **R-15 CTA repetition.** "Open the market" appears 3 times, plus a second hero button: the 2-CTA hero template.
15. **Focal point.** Sign and a white card stack in the center; the card covers the village and competes with the sign for attention.

## Proposed direction for the fix (content-driven, needs approval)

- Hero: village stays visible, sign swings once on load (no loop), headline and one CTA on a scrim, no card.
- Replace the numbered steps with one real deal from the feed, told start to finish: the player's offer, the merchant's actual reply from `/api/feed` chat, the signed deal with its explorer link. Evidence instead of claims.
- Add the identity motif: a chalk price board of the 12 goods, live from `/api/market`, with up/down marks that flash only when a price changes.
- Merchants as a varied row (portrait, voice line, live purse, their coin), coins folded in; drop the separate benefits and coins sections.
- Motion: one entrance per section heading, price flash on change, nothing looping.
